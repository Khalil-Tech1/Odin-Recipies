Ramen

Ramen is a popular Japanese noodle soup known for its rich broth and various toppings. It is often enjoyed as a hearty meal during colder months.

Ingredients:

  • Ramen Noodles
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Onion
  • Pepper
  • Vegetables
  • Broth (Chicken, Beef, or egg)

Steps:

  1. Boil water in a pot.
  2. Add Ramen noodles and cook for 3 minutes.
  3. Pour the broth into the pot and let it simmer.
  4. Drain the water and add vegetable oil, onion, and pepper.
  5. Mix well and serve hot with your choice of toppings.
