Indomie

Indomie is a popular Indonesian instant noodle brand known for its flavorful taste and convenience. It is often served with various toppings and sauces to create a delicious meal.

Ingredients:

  • Indomie Noodles
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Onion
  • Pepper
  • Vegetables

Steps:

  1. Boil water in a pot.
  2. Add Indomie noodles and cook for 3 minutes.
  3. Drain the water and add vegetable oil, onion, and pepper.
  4. Mix well and serve hot.
